# Notes: choices made where the brief was silent

- **One card per case.** The to-do list shows only the current step of each case, so people see what is theirs now. Case A also shows the travel expense (A4) as a side card once Legal has checked the rate.
- **Steps wait for Legal.** TalentBridge steps from the role check onward show "Locked: waiting for Legal to check the rate" until Legal has checked it. The button reads "This step is for Legal. Switch to Legal".
- **CloudDesk dispatch date.** The notice must be received by 7 Dec. The to-do card asks Procurement to dispatch 3 working days earlier (the courier time in the lead-time breakdown).
- **CloudDesk decisions.** "Renew as quoted" and "Don't renew" close or shorten the case and count no saving. Only a renegotiation counts value.
- **SwiftHaul.** The claim defaults to not accepting force majeure, because clause 7.3 needs evidence within 5 days and none was sent. Accepting it lowers the credit.
- **Brightline.** Two months of the Q4 discount are still ahead (November and December), which is how the 10-point Legal priority is worked out.
- **Excel tracker.** "Update the tracker from Docket" records the fix. A static site cannot edit a spreadsheet, so there is also "Copy corrected rows". The TalentBridge row waits until Legal has checked Amendment 2.
- **Value page.** One-off and yearly amounts are shown side by side and never added together. Repeat overcharges prevented and GST are shown apart from confirmed value.
- **Stage names.** CloudDesk and SwiftHaul items pass through the stage "Confirmed by Finance" as the spec's stage list names it, even when the person who confirmed was the business owner or Procurement.
- **Counts in assumption A1** come from the data (7 suppliers, 12 documents) rather than the 6 and 10 in the first draft.
- **Dark mode.** Status colours are lightened in dark mode so text stays above 4.5:1. Navy buttons become a lighter blue so they do not vanish into the page.
- **Law Insider check.** Sample contracts in the public categories are long and formal, with a dated preamble, named parties and recitals. Our documents are short clause extracts, so they read as plausible but not complete. Law Insider's "Subscription Agreement" samples are mostly investor share subscriptions, not software subscriptions like the CloudDesk agreement. Nothing was copied.
- **Tour.** Four chapters. Each step rings one area of the page and a docked guide explains it (a bottom sheet on phones), with a short chapter picker on a softly dimmed page. "Do it for me" calls the same store action the buttons call and says "Already done" if you got there first. Clicking a top-bar tab mid-tour asks "Leave the tour?".
- **Welcome and sign-in.** A first screen explains the idea (what happened, what should have happened, how Docket points it out), then a simulated sign-in (username and password are both test), then a choice of how to start. It is not real security and is labelled as simulated. Sign out returns to it.
- **Layout.** Pages use the full width on large screens. Item pages are two columns on laptops (what to do on the left, the proof on the right) and one column on phones.
- **Assumption chips.** Amber tags (A1 to A10) are used only for assumptions, so they never read as a status or a money gap.
- **Excel files.** Item pages, Contracts and Value offer CSV downloads that open in Excel. A static site can make CSV files in the browser without an extra library.
- **People.** All people use invented Indian names. The five role holders are Ritika Deshmukh (Finance), Rushikesh Kulkarni (Legal), Yash Patil (Procurement), Anuj Shinde (business owner) and Udit Pawar (TalentBridge billing). The timesheet name inside a recorded AI output was changed to match; the AI reasoning itself is unchanged.
- **Supplier portal.** A supplier signing in (Udit Pawar) gets a separate portal with no role switch and none of Larkspur's internal screens. It shows plain facts only: agreements and what each document says, purchase orders, the next end date, open invoices, credit notes and Larkspur contacts. The one active item is the email Larkspur sent about an invoice, with "Agree and issue credit note" or "Explain why we disagree". Staff cannot switch into it from the top bar; only the tour can show it.
- **Timeline.** The Timeline tab on Contracts shows every document as a bar on one axis (three months back to the end of the 25th month ahead), with icons for notice deadlines, auto-renewals, end dates, claim windows and signatures. Red means inside 30 days, amber inside 90, green later. All dates come from the contracts and the engine. Phones get the lists instead of the chart.
